  • Why against a year ago: listings follow the calendar — days on market fall every spring and rise every autumn — so a month compared with the month before mostly measures the season. The same month a year earlier removes it.
  • No blended score. This page used to rank states by a 0-100 "market climate" average of four Redfin figures from May 2026. Each measure now stands alone with its month; sort by the one you care about.
  • Redfin sales columns (sale price ÷ list price, months of supply) measure sales, which Realtor.com does not publish. Redfin's public data stopped after its {{ redfin_label }} window, so they are shown as of then.
Sources: Realtor.com Economic Research, Inventory Core Metrics (state and national, monthly); Redfin Data Center state market tracker (through {{ redfin_period_end or "—" }}). Listings, not sales: a list price is what sellers ask.
