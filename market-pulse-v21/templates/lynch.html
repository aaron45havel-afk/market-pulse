What this screen is, and what it deliberately does not do
  • Lynch's rule was PEG < 1. This board also demands a single-digit P/E, which is stricter and selects the corner of PEG < 1 where the market has priced the growth as fake. That is a deliberate value tilt, not Lynch's screen — expect turnarounds and companies the market distrusts, and read the last-year column before the CAGR.
  • A trough base is not a growth rate. Abercrombie went 4.20 → 0.05 → 6.22 → 10.69 and the screen printed 493.6%. When the base year is far below the window's median, or the latest year has no history behind it, no rate is reported at all.
  • Missing is never a pass. An unfiled debt tag used to read as zero debt, which is the best possible score on a gate that decides membership. Unmeasured now rejects with a named reason and appears in the funnel above.
  • Every ratio is bounded and shows its raw value on hover. Growth is capped, the P/E has a floor as well as a ceiling, ROE is capped as equity approaches zero.
  • Not here: forward earnings (Lynch's PEG used expected growth — every free version substitutes a trailing rate, which is exactly why the trough guard matters), institutional ownership, and daily volume. None is obtainable free from a CI runner.
