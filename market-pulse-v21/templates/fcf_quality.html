What this is, and what it deliberately does not do
  • The order is the method. Cheap on cash first, growing second. Rank on a blend of the two instead and a spectacular grower on a 1% yield walks into a screen whose entire premise is buying cash flow cheaply. Drop the second stage and you buy value traps — which is visible in the funnel above, where the cash-flow screen alone lowers the growth of what it keeps.
  • The proportions are VFLO's own — 75 of 400, then 50 of 75. Carried as fractions rather than counts, because this universe is not 400 and fixed counts would silently change how selective the screen is as coverage moved.
  • No forward estimates, and this is the real gap. VFLO averages trailing and forward 12-month free cash flow, and its growth score includes a 3–5 year consensus EPS growth estimate. Both need an analyst feed. Everything here comes from filings, so everything here is trailing. A company whose cash flow is about to fall off a cliff looks identical to one whose is about to double. The guards below catch bad data; nothing in a filing catches a bad future.
  • Missing is never a pass. A company whose debt tag did not file is not given zero debt — that would shrink its denominator, raise its yield, and push it up a board sorted by yield, so the company that failed to file would outrank the one that did. It is measured, counted, and left unranked with a reason.
  • One denominator for the whole board. Mixing a market-cap yield and an enterprise-value yield in one ranking compares two different quantities and hands an advantage to whichever rows are missing data. The cohort picks one basis; rows that cannot supply it are listed, not blended in.
  • A broken price is a bargain. Every multiple here is computed from market capitalisation rather than share price, because the share price in the source file is wrong for some tickers — Booking Holdings arrives at $193 against a real ~$4,500, which would print a 0.7x P/FCF and take the top of the board. On market cap it is 16.0x. Rows are additionally run through the same fault test the HOLT board uses, and flagged, not dropped.
  • Not here: sector caps, position weighting, or a 4% holding limit. VFLO is a fund and has to be investable; this is a screen and stops at selection. Their published final stage lifts yield again by weighting holdings by free cash flow — there is no fourth stage here to compare it to.
